# Acoustic kit credits

Virtuosity Drums, by Versilian Studios LLC and Karoryfer Lecolds, recorded at Virtuosity Musical Instruments, Boston. Released under CC0 1.0 Universal. License included in assets/formkit/audio/LICENSE.txt.

Primary source: https://versilian-studios.com/virtuosity-drums/
Source repository: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums/tree/9f04cf9a734527edfbb0a4eee1f674e45bbf71bc

The manifest at assets/formkit/audio/manifest.json records every original recording path and the pinned source commit. These are real acoustic recordings, converted with FFmpeg to mono 48 kHz PCM16 WAV; tails longer than six seconds are trimmed with a final 100ms fade. Playback normalizes peak levels, selects four snare velocity layers, alternates two recorded hits per voice/layer, applies velocity gain, and chokes open hats on a closed-hat hit. No third-party audio server is used at runtime. CC0 permits redistribution and adaptation.
